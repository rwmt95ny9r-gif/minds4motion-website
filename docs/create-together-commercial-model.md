# MINDS4MOTION — Create Together
## Commercial model / internal reference

Status: exploratory offer model  
Public website: no pricing shown  
Purpose: keep the full service logic, scope and pricing hypothesis retrievable in GitHub while the public site tests whether there is real demand.

---

## Positioning

Create Together is not positioned as classic coaching, management consulting or graphic design.

The common idea is:

> Think. Feel. Create.  
> Turning thoughts, feelings and fragments into something visible.

The first test is not revenue optimization. The first test is whether people voluntarily make contact because one of the three offer directions resonates with them.

The public site therefore intentionally avoids:
- package tables
- public pricing
- booking automation
- artificial scarcity
- aggressive sales language

The initial call to action is simply: **Tell me what’s moving you.**

# 01 — Creative Sparring

## Role in the portfolio

Primary entry offer.  
Lowest barrier to understand and easiest way to start a collaboration.

## Who it is for

People with:
- a thought they cannot yet structure
- an idea that still feels vague
- a personal or creative project without a clear direction
- several fragments that need to be connected
- a concept that needs an outside perspective before it moves forward

## Core promise

Bring structure into what is already there.

The work can include:
- clarifying the core idea
- connecting fragments and themes
- questioning assumptions
- sharpening language
- identifying visual or emotional references
- defining possible directions
- translating an abstract idea into concrete next steps

## Suggested format

- short written intake before the session
- 90-minute Creative Sparring session
- concise digital follow-up after the session
- follow-up can contain:
  - core idea
  - key themes
  - possible language
  - visual / music references where relevant
  - 2–5 recommended next steps

## Launch pricing hypothesis

**€290 per session including concise digital follow-up**

Test corridor: **€250–€350**

Reasoning:
- high enough to position it as meaningful professional creative work
- low enough for a first collaboration without a large commitment
- keeps the model compatible with limited available time
- creates a useful benchmark before building larger packages

## Out of scope

Unless explicitly agreed:
- ongoing coaching
- therapy
- full graphic design production
- full brand implementation
- website development
- copywriting of large deliverables
- unlimited revisions

# 02 — Personal Story / Soundtrack

## Role in the portfolio

Emotional / personal MINDS4MOTION offer.

## Who it is for

People who want to give a personal chapter a form:
- a transition
- a relationship or important person
- a set of songs
- meaningful places
- a memory
- a period of change
- an inner state that is difficult to describe directly

## Core promise

Turn personal meaning into a curated creative concept.

This is deliberately not a biography.  
It is closer to an emotional portrait.

Possible ingredients:
- music
- words
- images
- places
- memories
- colors
- fragments
- personal symbols

Possible outputs:
- personal soundtrack concept
- visual story
- digital artwork direction
- written / visual chapter
- small creative digital piece combining text, image and music references

## Suggested format

Stage 1:
- personal intake / conversation
- 60–90 minute exploration session

Stage 2:
- creative interpretation and curation

Stage 3:
- presentation of the resulting concept
- one refinement loop

## Launch pricing hypothesis

**€490 for a compact personal concept**

Working corridor depending on depth and output: **€300–€800**

A larger bespoke piece should be quoted individually.

## Important principle

The value is not AI image generation.

The value is:
- listening
- selection
- interpretation
- curation
- connecting personal fragments
- translating them into one coherent creative expression

# 03 — Creative Concepting

## Role in the portfolio

Higher-value B2B / founder / project offer.

## Who it is for

People or small teams with:
- an early business idea
- a brand thought
- a new project
- an unclear proposition
- a concept that feels promising but fragmented
- a pitch that lacks narrative coherence

## Core promise

Give an idea a clearer story, identity and first form.

Possible workstreams:
- core idea
- problem framing
- target audience
- positioning
- value proposition
- naming direction
- narrative
- visual language
- creative references
- first pitch structure
- concept architecture

## Suggested format

Discovery:
- written context
- 60–90 minute working session

Concept phase:
- synthesis
- creative exploration
- development of 1–3 coherent directions

Output:
- compact concept document / deck
- recommendation
- optional refinement session

## Launch pricing hypothesis

**from €1,200 per defined concept project**

Typical experimental corridor:
- compact concept sprint: **€1,200–€1,800**
- broader concept / positioning / narrative package: **€1,800–€3,000+**

Pricing should remain project-based rather than hourly wherever possible.

## Out of scope unless separately agreed

- legal naming / trademark clearance
- full corporate identity
- production-ready design systems
- complete website implementation
- media buying
- ongoing marketing operations
- large-scale market research

# Portfolio logic

The three offers should not be presented as equal-sized commercial products.

Recommended hierarchy:

1. **Creative Sparring** — primary entry point
2. **Personal Story / Soundtrack** — emotional specialist offer
3. **Creative Concepting** — selective higher-value project work

This hierarchy keeps the public offer understandable while allowing very different types of inquiries.

---

# First demand test

Do not optimize the model before real inquiries exist.

For each inquiry, record:
- which offer triggered the contact
- what wording the person used to describe the problem
- how they found MINDS4MOTION
- expected output
- willingness to pay
- estimated effort
- actual effort
- whether the work felt energizing or draining
- whether the client would recommend it

After approximately 5 serious inquiries or 3 completed paid projects, review:
- offer names
- pricing
- scope
- most requested outcome
- time economics
- whether one offer should be removed or expanded

---

# Commercial principle

MINDS4MOTION itself should remain a creative space.

The commercial layer sits at the edge of it:

**MINDS4MOTION → Create Together → conversation → selectively defined work**

No need to turn the main site into a conventional service website.
